## The concept

**Big idea:** The site is a climb. Every move to a bigger market is a higher camp, and the summit is never reached, because you're still going.

**Thread:** Work hard vs work smart. You grew up hearing that hard work and study are enough. You learned that's only half the answer, and the site keeps showing both halves.

### The four camps

| Camp | Place | Years | What it gave you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base Camp | Hòa Bình | Childhood to 2023 | 12 years in gifted schools. Discipline, curiosity, a proper nerd. |
| Camp 1 | Hanoi | 2023 to 2025 | First real work: admissions, competitions, leading a club. |
| Camp 2 | Singapore | 2025 | A bigger market. High-stakes conversations with HNWI clients. |
| Camp 3 | Melbourne | 2025 to now | Agency life, AI, and finding out you are actually a quick learner after all. |
| Summit | Not yet | Next | The next climb. This is where the CTA lives. |

### Story arc (use it on every page)

1. Where I started: a small mountain city, a family belief in hard work, 12 years of gifted school.
2. What I learned: hard work isn't the whole story. Smart work is.
3. The doubt: "I'm not smart enough for tech. Not smart enough for AI."
4. The proof: global #1 at an AI build week, 26 client accounts, three countries.
5. What's next: always the bigger market.

### Voice

Bold, short sentences. First person. Confident, not cocky. Use climbing words sparingly (camp, trail, summit, view). Once per section is plenty. Every claim lands with a number.
